01 — About me

Backend engineer at heart.
AI builder by passion.

I'm a Python developer with 4+ years of hands-on experience building scalable backend systems, RESTful APIs and database-driven applications — and, more recently, production-grade Generative AI products.

My work spans the full backend lifecycle: modelling data in PostgreSQL and MongoDB, designing clean APIs with Django REST Framework and FastAPI, running background jobs with Celery & Redis, pushing real-time updates over WebSockets, and deploying to AWS and DigitalOcean behind Nginx and Gunicorn.

On the AI side, I turn LLMs into reliable product features — RAG pipelines, agents and chatbots built with LangChain, CrewAI, OpenAI and Google Gemini — for use cases ranging from legal research and document intelligence to personalised meal and workout planning.

I work closely with cross-functional teams, care about clean architecture and performance, and consistently ship high-quality solutions on deadline.
